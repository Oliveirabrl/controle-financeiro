Frequently asked questions

Why on a computer and not on a phone?

So your finances stay with you: the program works without internet, sign-up or cloud, and your data never leaves your computer. On a big screen you see the month, your cards and your debt forecast at a glance. It runs on a desktop or laptop with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); there is no Mac or phone version.

Which currency?

The first time you open the program, you choose US dollars (US$), Paraguayan guaraní (₲) or Brazilian reais (R$). You can change it later in Your brand.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the license is for life.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the installer. After that, the program works offline.

I see “Windows protected your PC”. What now?

That's normal for programs from independent developers. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

Can I use it on two computers?

Each license is for one computer. For another one, buy an additional license.

If I reformat my computer, do I lose everything?

Make a backup first (Backup button). Then send the new computer code and the license is reissued: free in the first 90 days and US$ 6 after that.

What does support include?

For the first 90 days, in writing: fixing program defects, help installing and activating the license, and license reissue. It doesn't include teaching how to use the features, training, help entering your data, video calls, remote access or problems with your computer or Windows. To learn how to use it, the program has the Help screen and the demo with sample data.

Does it work for a loan from a relative with no set payment?

Yes. Add it as “Loan with no fixed payment, with monthly interest” (the rate can be zero) and record each payment with the amount you paid.

Does it tell me where to invest?

No. It shows whether your budget, debts and emergency fund are ready for you to think about investing, but it doesn't recommend products or give advice. The decisions are yours.

What if I don't like it?

You can cancel within 7 days and get a full refund through the same payment method.
